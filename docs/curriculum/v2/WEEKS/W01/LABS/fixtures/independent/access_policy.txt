Synthetic access policy for the audit-intake service on G9-TGT-01 (lab file, no real data). Version 1.

Principals
  labadmin   administrator account of the guest; may use sudo for any command.
  svcaudit   service account; runs the audit-intake service; no login shell.
  labuser3   auditor; member of group auditors.
  labuser4   operator; member of group ops.

Required access
  R1  The service runs as svcaudit and has no privilege beyond that account.
  R2  The service listens on 127.0.0.1 port 8090 and on no other address.
  R3  Auditors may read the records in /srv/g9toy-audit/spool. Nobody except the service account may change them.
  R4  Operators may restart the service. Operators may not read or change records. An operator may place one file named requeue_ops.txt in the spool directory.
  R5  Only labuser3, labuser4 and labadmin may log in over SSH, with a key. Password logins are not allowed. Root may not log in with a password.
  R6  A secret used by the service is not visible to an unprivileged account.
