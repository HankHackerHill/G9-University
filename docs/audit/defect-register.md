# Defect and correction register (Stage 00, v6.1)

These are **design-review findings**, not observed defects in running software. Rows C01–C22 are the v6.0 corrections to v5.1 (preserved verbatim in intent). Rows TEC/SEC/LRN/CON are the independent Stage 00 auditors' findings over both documents (technical, adversarial-security, learning-science, consistency), produced on 2026-11-05/06. The planned skeptical verification pass and the platform/licensing and improvement auditors did not complete (usage limit); every auditor row is therefore labeled **AUDITOR-REPORTED (not independently verified)** unless its Status says otherwise. v6.1 (this repository: `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/adr/`) supersedes v5.1 and v6.0 where they conflict.
