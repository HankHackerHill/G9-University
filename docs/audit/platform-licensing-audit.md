## Narrative

The platform choice holds for 26.04: `libwebkit2gtk-4.1` is packaged for resolute, Tauri 2.12.5 is the current stable 2.x line, and the CI label is GA. What fails today is the supply-chain gate, not the platform. The three `cargo-deny` failures are configuration defects: missing `targets`, missing `MIT-0`, and crates not marked `publish = false`. The fourth is a known unmaintained proc-macro in the GTK3 stack that needs a reasoned ignore. The `npm audit` failure is a one-line vitest bump. All four will block CI on the first run. The Tauri shell is the larger gap: `ARCHITECTURE.md` and the triple audit describe a CSP and a capability allow-list that do not exist as files, and the shell cannot build. Under the CLAUDE.md integrity rules those statements must be PROPOSED until the files exist.

The licensing exposure lies outside the code. Model weights and external training platforms carry use restrictions that collide with this product's purpose. AUP-bearing community licences prohibit malicious-code generation that the curriculum's malware, privilege-escalation and red-team weeks need. PortSwigger's terms prohibit commercial use, and HTB and CyberDefenders restrict publication — while CT9 is a business with an export skill (K03). Both belong in `g9-policy` and `model_registry` as data with enforcement, not as notes.

For the RTX 5080 host the binding constraints are: the open kernel module only; CUDA ≥ 12.8 for native sm_120; a VRAM budget shared with KWin and WebKitGTK; and KV-cache size, which caps a 14B model near 16k context. None of these is measured yet. The benchmark protocol must record them before any candidate is compared.
