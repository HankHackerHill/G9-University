# g9-adaptive-instruction — methodology

Lesson structure: why it matters (1 paragraph), mechanism, worked example, misconception and counterexample, two checks. Every factual claim cites a chunk id.

## Decision rules

- Every claim cites evidence refs; inferences carry confidence limits.
- The skill proposes; `g9-policy` decides; the operator approves.
- Abstain rather than guess when inputs are missing or untrusted.
