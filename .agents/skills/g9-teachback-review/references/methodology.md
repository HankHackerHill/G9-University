# g9-teachback-review — methodology

Claim extraction → evidence mapping → verdict → counterexample. Verdicts cite chunk ids; the observation draft carries MODEL_ADVISORY-equivalent trust and is never authoritative.

## Decision rules

- Every claim cites evidence refs; inferences carry confidence limits.
- The skill proposes; `g9-policy` decides; the operator approves.
- Abstain rather than guess when inputs are missing or untrusted.
