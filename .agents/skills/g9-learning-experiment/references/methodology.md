# g9-learning-experiment — methodology

Designs: objective-within-day randomization, counterbalanced day pairs, multiple baseline across clusters. Predeclared outcomes, randomization/bootstrap intervals, inconclusive allowed, no fixed thresholds.

## Decision rules

- Every claim cites evidence refs; inferences carry confidence limits.
- The skill proposes; `g9-policy` decides; the operator approves.
- Abstain rather than guess when inputs are missing or untrusted.
