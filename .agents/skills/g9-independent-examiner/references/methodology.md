# g9-independent-examiner — methodology

Anchored levels 0–4 per criterion; weights per rubric; MODEL_ADVISORY results never contribute to a final decision (C09). Exposure of any task content is logged.

## Decision rules

- Every claim cites evidence refs; inferences carry confidence limits.
- The skill proposes; `g9-policy` decides; the operator approves.
- Abstain rather than guess when inputs are missing or untrusted.
