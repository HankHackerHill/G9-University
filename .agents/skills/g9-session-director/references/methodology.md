# g9-session-director — methodology

Brief order: objectives → prerequisite status → due reviews → open interventions → time budget → risks → next step. Only recorded evidence; no praise, no invented numbers.

## Decision rules

- Every claim cites evidence refs; inferences carry confidence limits.
- The skill proposes; `g9-policy` decides; the operator approves.
- Abstain rather than guess when inputs are missing or untrusted.
