# g9-ct9-knowledge-export — methodology

Eligibility = approved reference or operator-approved deliverable AND license permits redistribution AND classification PUBLIC/INTERNAL. Manifest carries sha256 per file.

## Decision rules

- Every claim cites evidence refs; inferences carry confidence limits.
- The skill proposes; `g9-policy` decides; the operator approves.
- Abstain rather than guess when inputs are missing or untrusted.
