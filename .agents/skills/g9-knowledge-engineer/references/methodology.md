# g9-knowledge-engineer — methodology

Chunk record: source id, version, trust class, classification, sha256, freshness date. Retrieval answers cite chunk ids; claims are separated from validated truth.

## Decision rules

- Every claim cites evidence refs; inferences carry confidence limits.
- The skill proposes; `g9-policy` decides; the operator approves.
- Abstain rather than guess when inputs are missing or untrusted.
