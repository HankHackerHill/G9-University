# g9-learning-data-model — methodology

Four dimensions; assistance weights; difficulty 1–5; validity windows; Regress retains earlier evidence; corrections supersede; derivation is a pure fold.

## Decision rules

- Every claim cites evidence refs; inferences carry confidence limits.
- The skill proposes; `g9-policy` decides; the operator approves.
- Abstain rather than guess when inputs are missing or untrusted.
